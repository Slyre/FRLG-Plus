            Field notes

            Play at your own pace.

            01

            Meet up with your partner

            Both players run play.bat on their own PC. One creates a room and shares the code; the other joins with that code. Load your saves and leave battles, menus and dialogue until the duo is ready. No IDs to enter in Pokémon.

            02

            Keep playing your way

            Captures, evolutions and deaths are tracked automatically. You can battle separately. Readings travel in the background. Each game applies updates when it is free; neither player needs to wait for the other to finish a battle.

            Death reports travel separately and apply when the receiving game is free. Restarting or disabling Nuzlocke does not bring a dead pair back.

            03

            Choose your team together

            In Choose your team, either player can propose pairs. The other accepts or declines. Once you agree, the games move Pokémon in or out of the PC. Leave enough PC space and remove held mail that blocks a transfer.

            A lone capture waits for its partner. Dead pairs cannot be selected. A starter received before connecting can be recovered if it is still in your party and its origin identifies it unambiguously.

            Alerts

            New proposals and lost pairs appear in the journal. If this tab is in the background, its title shows an unread count. Sound and desktop alerts are optional and off when you open the journal.

            What does Relay mean?

            The number is the latest round trip to the server. It is shown only while connected and recently measured. An update can still wait for a game to leave a battle or menu.

            Something missing?

            Click Sync. One click checks both games. The HP shown comes from the last reading outside battle.

            Closed this tab?

            The game keeps running. companion.bat reopens the journal. Keep the launcher console open and save in Pokémon before quitting.

            Starting over?

            Use two fresh saves and a new room code. A linked save keeps its original duo. Close both games before updating and keep your .sav.

            Using the journal with a keyboard

            Tab moves between controls; Space selects a pair. Use the arrow keys, Home or End on the page buttons to switch pages. Your selection stays focused when readings update.

            Keyboard and controller

            In mGBA: Tools → Settings → Keyboard or Controllers. Change rules in the Soul Link menu in each game.

            Your save stays on your PC. Remember to save in Pokémon.
